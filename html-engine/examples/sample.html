What we'll cover

Three questions, in order

  • The approach — why one clean pass beats many partial sweeps

The claim

A survey flown in one pass is honest about its blind spots.

Repeated sweeps hide their gaps in the overlap. One pass shows exactly where the signal ends — the most useful line on the chart.
